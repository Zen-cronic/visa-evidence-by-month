# Common-law rules

Apply these rules only when `{RELATIONSHIP}` is common-law, for a couple or a family. SKILL.md points here at Step 6. For every other case, skip this file.

The same-address rules in [same-address.md](same-address.md) apply to common-law partners too: `{PARTNERS}`, the single-name papers at an address (`{ADDRESS_DOCS}`), same-address pairs, `{SAME_ADDRESS_DOCS}`, drops, the comparison and the paper copies. This file holds only what is different for common-law.

## Step 6: coverage, gaps and footer

When `{RELATIONSHIP}` is common-law, a month counts as covered only when the partners are shown at the same address: by one paper naming both `{PARTNERS}` with an address, or by a same-address pair (see [same-address.md](same-address.md)). Photos are shown but do not count, and papers naming both partners without an address are listed but do not count.

When `{RELATIONSHIP}` is common-law, add a fourth column, "Same address shown (counts)", naming the paper or papers that show it, and a gap is a month where the partners are not shown at the same address, whatever the photos. Group consecutive gap months and label them "no paper showing you both at the same address found: April to June 2026" (the months as they apply). The gap rule and gap label for everyone else in SKILL.md Step 6, and the married-or-other rule in same-address.md, do not apply. This overrides every other gap, gap-label, check-by-hand and face-grouping-miss sentence in SKILL.md and the references, including when the photo search failed, was skipped or left a month unresolved: for common-law, a month with no paper showing both partners at the same address is a gap, and no photo or check-by-hand line can change that.

What could cover each gap: for common-law, a paper from `{LINKED_DOCS}` counts in a series, or as the paper that starts after the gap, only if it names both partners with an address; papers from `{ADDRESS_DOCS}` count as same-address.md says.

Under the gap list, always add, when it is common-law: "This table shows which months have papers showing you both at the same address. It does not show whether you meet the 12-month rule; check the official rules or ask a licensed consultant. Photos and other papers stay in the pack; they just do not fill a month for this check."
